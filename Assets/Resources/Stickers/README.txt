Place sticker PNG files in this folder. The sticker picker loads PNG textures from Assets/Resources/Stickers.
The Custom menu contains both the paint and sticker panels. Starting Custom locks weapon construction until Custom is removed; removing Custom clears all paint and stickers and unlocks construction.
On narrow screens, Paint and Stickers switch to tabs so the panels do not overlap or clip. The sticker list has a visible vertical scrollbar.
Transparent PNG backgrounds are supported. Restart the scene after adding or removing stickers.
The default sticker size has a maximum dimension of 1.275 module units (1.25x the previous size), with the PNG aspect ratio preserved.
Sticker parts are clipped to the continuous coplanar surface of the assembled weapon. Adjacent block faces stay connected; gaps and outer edges are clipped.
To remove a sticker, activate the sticker eraser in the right-side sticker panel and click the sticker. Undo restores removed stickers.
